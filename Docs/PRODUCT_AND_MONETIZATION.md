# Product strategy and monetisation

## Product promise

**Understand what you are trying to achieve, what to do next, what you actually did, and which capabilities you built—without losing the story when life changes.**

The durable skill history is the differentiator. Conventional task managers optimise task throughput; habit apps optimise streaks; goal trackers optimise a target snapshot; role-playing productivity apps often optimise game rewards. SkillTree IRL connects changing goals to immutable evidence-backed effort and permanent skills.

## Who should use it

Primary users are self-directed adults managing several evolving areas of life: learning, health, career, creative work, finances, relationships, and long projects. They want structure and reflection but reject guilt-heavy streak systems. The product is particularly strong for people whose goals change over several years and who still value the skills acquired along the way.

It is not positioned as project management for teams, clinical treatment, financial advice, a social leaderboard, or an AI coach that must be trusted blindly.

## Retention loop

The sustainable loop is:

1. **Orient:** Today shows a single next-best action and current focus.
2. **Act:** the user completes a quest/habit, focuses, or records work.
3. **Confirm:** activity, evidence, progress, and skill XP become a trustworthy record.
4. **Understand:** weekly insight reveals momentum without shame.
5. **Accumulate:** the SkillTree and history become more valuable over months and years.

Notifications support this loop but do not manufacture urgency. The core retention asset is accumulated personal history, portable through export and never held hostage by a paid tier.

## Free plan - £0 forever

Free must be good enough to create genuine habit and trust:

- full onboarding, Today, goals, skills, quests, habits, activities, focus sessions, journal, history, achievements, seasons, reminders, privacy, export, and deletion;
- up to ten Active/Focus goals, with unlimited completed/paused/archived history;
- daily and weekly insight;
- 25 MB private evidence;
- no time limit and no XP/level disadvantage.

This boundary lets a normal individual use SkillTree indefinitely. It also creates a meaningful dataset and a reason to return before asking for payment.

## Pro plan - £7.99/month

Pro starts with a 14-day trial and is aimed at power users whose history is now valuable:

- unlimited active goals;
- 30-day momentum and goal-movement analysis;
- completion forecast ranges and strongest-skill trends;
- year reviews and long-range analysis;
- imports and provider integrations;
- reusable custom templates;
- developer API keys and outbound webhooks;
- 250 MB private evidence.

The product catalogue, pricing page, checkout, webhook entitlements, account screen, and customer portal must all describe the same tier. Any future annual price should be added only when its Stripe Price and refund/cancellation wording are configured consistently.

## Conversion moments

Offer Pro after value is visible, not during the first empty screen:

- when the user opens advanced Insights after building enough history;
- when creating an eleventh active goal;
- when requesting a year review;
- when importing existing history or connecting a provider;
- when creating reusable templates or developer integrations;
- when evidence storage approaches the free allowance.

Every paywall explains what remains free and links back to a useful action. Never block access to previously created history because a subscription ended.

## Ethical and commercial guardrails

- Never sell XP, achievements, streak repair, ranking advantage, or essential privacy/security.
- Never use missed-day shame, fabricated scarcity, hidden cancellation, or a misleading trial.
- Entitlements are backend-authoritative and removed after the paid/trial period ends.
- Billing failures do not delete data; the account reverts to Free limits while retaining history.
- Do not enable Stripe Tax until registrations are active in the relevant jurisdictions.
- Prices and product claims must be updated together in code, Stripe, legal terms, and support documentation.

## Product success metrics

Track privacy-safe events rather than personal goal text:

- activation: onboarding completed and first real activity within 24 hours;
- weekly retained use: at least one meaningful activity on two distinct days;
- next-action usefulness: recommended action opened/completed;
- durable value: users returning to history/SkillTree after 30 and 90 days;
- trust: successful exports, low duplicate/reversal rate, evidence failures, deletion completion;
- conversion: qualified paywall view to trial, trial to paid, paid retention, and voluntary cancellation reasons;
- guardrails: reminder opt-outs, support complaints, payment failures, and privacy/security incidents.

Analytics must avoid storing sensitive descriptions, journal text, evidence, financial amounts, or location where not essential.
