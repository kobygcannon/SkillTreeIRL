# Product strategy and monetisation

## Product promise

**Understand what you are trying to achieve, what to do next, what you actually did, and which capabilities you built—without losing the story when life changes.**

The durable skill history is the differentiator. Conventional task managers optimise task throughput; habit apps optimise streaks; goal trackers optimise a target snapshot; role-playing productivity apps often optimise game rewards. SkillTree IRL connects changing goals to immutable evidence-backed effort and permanent skills.

## Who should use it

Primary users are self-directed adults managing several evolving areas of life: learning, health, career, creative work, finances, relationships, and long projects. They want structure and reflection but reject guilt-heavy streak systems. The product is particularly strong for people whose goals change over several years and who still value the skills acquired along the way.

It is not positioned as project management for teams, clinical treatment, financial advice, a social leaderboard, or an AI coach that must be trusted blindly.

### Priority customer groups

| Customer | Job they hire SkillTree to do | Why existing tools fail them | Return pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ambitious individual | Turn several life ambitions into one manageable next step | Tasks lose the reason; habit apps punish missed days | Brief daily check-in, weekly review |
| Learner or career changer | Prove what they practised and see transferable skills accumulate | Courses show completion, not durable capability | After each learning session, monthly portfolio review |
| Creator or sole trader | Connect product, audience, sales and learning work to longer-term capability | Project tools fragment work by project and client | Daily action capture, weekly business reflection |
| Coach-supported individual | Keep a private record between sessions and bring useful evidence to reviews | Notes and spreadsheets do not reveal momentum | Self-directed daily use, shared review when chosen |
| Small company | Align development objectives, assignments and check-ins without exposing private personal goals | HR suites are heavy; task tools do not model development | Manager weekly check-in, member daily/weekly updates |

The launch wedge is the ambitious individual, especially learners, career changers, creators and sole traders. Company workspaces are a separate context with roles, invitations, objectives, assignments and check-ins; they must never turn a personal SkillTree into employer surveillance.

## Why somebody chooses and keeps it

The reason to try is immediate clarity: one goal becomes one editable, recommended quest in under three minutes. The reason to return tomorrow is that Today reduces re-planning to a single next-best action. The reason to keep it for a year is the connected record—actions, evidence, goals and permanent skills remain understandable even after a goal changes or ends.

This creates four defensible product advantages:

1. **Goals are temporary; skills are durable.** Changing direction does not erase the value of earlier work.
2. **Progress is evidence, not decoration.** XP is awarded by real activity and can be reversed; it cannot be bought.
3. **The interface collapses complexity.** New users see one focus and one suggested quest, while deeper planning remains available on demand.
4. **Momentum is compassionate and truthful.** Missing a day does not destroy progress or trigger shame. The product helps the user restart.

## Retention loop

The sustainable loop is:

1. **Orient:** Today shows a single next-best action and current focus.
2. **Act:** the user completes a quest/habit, focuses, or records work.
3. **Confirm:** activity, evidence, progress, and skill XP become a trustworthy record.
4. **Understand:** weekly insight reveals momentum without shame.
5. **Accumulate:** the SkillTree and history become more valuable over months and years.

Notifications support this loop but do not manufacture urgency. The core retention asset is accumulated personal history, portable through export and never held hostage by a paid tier.

### Experience standards

- Onboarding should take 1–3 minutes and create a Focus goal. The first Today view generates a concrete suggested quest that can be edited, accepted or dismissed.
- A returning user should be able to complete the highlighted quest, mark a habit, start a focus session or open activity logging with one primary decision.
- The useful daily interaction can take seconds. Planning depth is optional rather than forced before progress can be recorded.
- Empty states always offer a meaningful next action; they never present a wall of empty charts.
- Mobile keeps Today, quick add and completion within thumb reach. Desktop exposes planning and analysis without changing the underlying mental model.
- Undo/reversal is part of trust: accidental progress must be correctable without corrupting XP or history.

### Habit formation without manipulation

- Daily: one recommended action, quick completion, immediate progress/skill confirmation.
- Weekly: activity days, XP, goals moved, strongest recent skill and next focus.
- Monthly: 30-day momentum, goal health and completion ranges when enough evidence exists.
- Yearly: a review of durable skills and completed work, reserved for Pro because it requires long-range analysis—not because the underlying history is withheld.

Reminders are opt-in and timezone-aware. A missed period changes the recommendation to a small restart action; it never removes XP, fakes urgency or sells streak repair.

## Insight model

Stats must answer a decision, not merely decorate the dashboard:

| Signal | User question answered | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today: activities, quests, habits and XP | “Did I move anything today?” | Free |
| Weekly active days and goals moved | “Am I acting consistently and on what?” | Free summary |
| Lifetime actions, XP, level and discovered skills | “What have I built over time?” | Free |
| 30-day active days and recent activity volume | “Is my momentum strengthening or cooling?” | Pro |
| Strongest recent skills | “Which capabilities am I actually practising?” | Pro |
| Completion forecast range | “At my observed pace, when might this finish?” | Pro, only after enough evidence |
| Year review and long-range patterns | “How did my direction and capabilities change?” | Pro |
| Company objective/check-in status | “Where is support or clarification needed?” | Company, scoped by role |

Forecasts must use ranges and explain sample size and observed pace. No screen should imply causal certainty, clinical assessment or employee performance scoring.

## Free plan - £0 forever

Free must be good enough to create genuine habit and trust:

- full onboarding, Today, goals, skills, quests, habits, activities, focus sessions, journal, history, achievements, seasons, reminders, privacy, export, and deletion;
- up to ten Active/Focus goals, with unlimited completed/paused/archived history;
- daily and weekly insight;
- 25 MB private evidence;
- no time limit and no XP/level disadvantage.

This boundary lets a normal individual use SkillTree indefinitely. It also creates a meaningful dataset and a reason to return before asking for payment.

## Pro plan - £7.99/month

Pro starts with a 14-day trial and is aimed at power users whose history is now valuable:

- unlimited active goals;
- 30-day momentum and goal-movement analysis;
- completion forecast ranges and strongest-skill trends;
- year reviews and long-range analysis;
- imports and provider integrations;
- reusable custom templates;
- developer API keys and outbound webhooks;
- 250 MB private evidence.

The product catalogue, pricing page, checkout, webhook entitlements, account screen, and customer portal must all describe the same tier. Any future annual price should be added only when its Stripe Price and refund/cancellation wording are configured consistently.

## Conversion moments

Offer Pro after value is visible, not during the first empty screen:

- when the user opens advanced Insights after building enough history;
- when creating an eleventh active goal;
- when requesting a year review;
- when importing existing history or connecting a provider;
- when creating reusable templates or developer integrations;
- when evidence storage approaches the free allowance.

Every paywall explains what remains free and links back to a useful action. Never block access to previously created history because a subscription ended.

### Why Pro is worth paying for

Free answers “what should I do and what did I build?” Pro answers “what pattern is emerging, what is likely next, and how can I reuse or connect my system?” The upgrade is attractive after history exists because forecasts, long-range reviews, imports, integrations, templates and automation save time or reveal patterns. It does not make XP faster or make Free intentionally frustrating.

### Why Company is distinct

Company is not Pro with extra seats. It adds a managed workspace, member roles, invitations, shared objectives, assignments, check-ins and subscription seats, with a hard boundary from each person’s private SkillTree. The buyer pays for coordination, access control and a reliable development cadence. The product must help managers support work, not secretly monitor private activity.

Company onboarding therefore asks for workspace purpose and team structure, then guides the owner to create an objective, invite members and schedule the first check-in. Members receive a role-specific join path and see only the company context they are permitted to access.

The Company workspace begins with one 14-day collaboration trial. Trial expiry makes shared workflows read-only without deleting company history or touching personal SkillTrees. Activating Stripe carries forward only the remaining trial period, preventing repeated or stacked trials. Paid value is therefore real coordination and controlled collaboration, not access to data already created.

## Ethical and commercial guardrails

- Never sell XP, achievements, streak repair, ranking advantage, or essential privacy/security.
- Never use missed-day shame, fabricated scarcity, hidden cancellation, or a misleading trial.
- Entitlements are backend-authoritative and removed after the paid/trial period ends.
- Billing failures do not delete data; the account reverts to Free limits while retaining history.
- Do not enable Stripe Tax until registrations are active in the relevant jurisdictions.
- Prices and product claims must be updated together in code, Stripe, legal terms, and support documentation.

## Product success metrics

Track privacy-safe events rather than personal goal text:

- activation: onboarding completed and first real activity within 24 hours;
- weekly retained use: at least one meaningful activity on two distinct days;
- next-action usefulness: recommended action opened/completed;
- durable value: users returning to history/SkillTree after 30 and 90 days;
- trust: successful exports, low duplicate/reversal rate, evidence failures, deletion completion;
- conversion: qualified paywall view to trial, trial to paid, paid retention, and voluntary cancellation reasons;
- guardrails: reminder opt-outs, support complaints, payment failures, and privacy/security incidents.

Analytics must avoid storing sensitive descriptions, journal text, evidence, financial amounts, or location where not essential.

### Launch decision thresholds

Use these as initial hypotheses, then revise from real cohorts rather than presenting them as promises:

- activation: at least 60% of verified signups create a goal and record a meaningful action within 24 hours;
- first-value speed: median under 3 minutes from onboarding start to a visible suggested quest;
- week-one retention: at least 30% of activated individuals record activity on two separate days in the first week;
- recommendation usefulness: at least 40% of displayed quest suggestions are accepted or edited-and-accepted;
- four-week retained use: at least 20% of activated individuals are active in week four;
- qualified Pro trial conversion: 5–10% of users who encounter a value-relevant Pro surface start a trial;
- trial-to-paid: at least 35%, with cancellation and refund friction monitored separately;
- trust guardrails: zero unauthorised cross-user/workspace reads, and successful export/deletion completion above 99%.

These measures should be segmented by individual and company journeys. Do not optimise raw daily opens: a person who completes meaningful work twice a week may be healthier and more successful than somebody opening a dashboard every day without acting.

## Product operating test

Before shipping a feature, answer all five questions:

1. Does it help the user choose, do, record, understand or accumulate meaningful progress?
2. Is its value clear without a tutorial or manipulative urgency?
3. Does it preserve user control, private history and reversibility?
4. Does it improve a defined product signal without collecting sensitive content?
5. If paid, is the value advanced capability or saved time rather than an artificial handicap on Free?

If the answer to any question is no, the feature is not ready for the product.
